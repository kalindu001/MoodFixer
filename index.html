A Message from the Heart of Mood Fixer

Life can feel really tough sometimes.. You don't have to deal with it by yourself. Mood Fixer is here to help. We want to meet you where you're right now and help you feel better.

Whether you're starting a day getting ready for bed or just need something to brighten up a tough moment we're here to help. We do this one message at a time.

We use a mix of computer technology and human emotions to turn your feelings into something. A small smile, a boost of energy or a funny moment can make a difference in your year. This is more than a website. It's a place where you can feel safe and supported.


"We think that the right words, at the time can really change how you feel. And even change your life."

— Created by P.R.K.I. Pallegama


Why Mood Fixer Matters

  • Instant Connection: You can tell us how you're feeling with emojis or honest words.
  • Pure Impact: We keep it short and sweet. A sentence or two that can make a big difference.
  • A Brighter Perspective: We want you to leave our site feeling better and happier.
  • Your Journey, Honored: We care about your progress and celebrate every day you choose to be happy.
